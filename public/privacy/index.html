SITE INFORMATION

Privacy, plainly.

Updated 8 October 2026. This policy describes the current website.

This site

This site has no accounts or contact forms. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after you select Allow analytics. Your choice is stored in your browser so we can remember it. The hosting provider may process routine connection information such as IP addresses and requested pages.

The embedded itch.io game

Selecting Play or Reload game loads the game from itch.io hosting. This sends requests to itch.io and its hosting domains, which can receive your IP address and browser information and apply their own privacy practices. Read itch.io’s privacy policy.

External links

Opening the developer page or a mobile store takes you to an external service. That service’s rules and privacy policy apply. This site does not receive your gameplay, purchase details or login credentials from those services.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google receives usage information such as page visits, device and browser details, and clicks on our Play and Reload buttons. Analytics may use cookies to measure visits. We do not send gameplay inside the embedded game to Google Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our tag. You can decline analytics and still play, or change your choice using Analytics settings in the footer. Previously collected data is not deleted when you withdraw consent. Google privacy policy.

Future advertising

Advertising is not active. This policy and any required consent controls must be updated before analytics or advertising is introduced.
