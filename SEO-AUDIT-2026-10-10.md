HTTP and www requests resolve to the canonical HTTPS bare domain. A deliberately nonexistent path returned HTTP 404, not a soft-404 homepage. Hostinger identifies creation as 8 October 2026.

The release adds 32 translated pages to the eight English pages. All 40 pages passed internal-link/asset existence, one-H1, JSON-LD parsing, unique self-canonical and reciprocal hreflang checks. The original English game is explicitly distinguished from translated site instructions.
